# 2. CORE INVARIANTS

The following invariants are mandatory audit targets.

## 2.1 Ownership

Ownership != Management.

A Company managing an Owner's property must NOT become the owner.

Ownership must be determined from authoritative persisted relationships.

Client-supplied ownerId must never override server-side authorization.

---

## 2.2 Management Authority

A Company may manage an Owner's property only when a valid management relationship/agreement and required permissions authorize the action.

Audit separately:

- property management
- tenant management
- listing authority
- financial management
- rent collection
- approval authority
- spending limits

Do NOT treat organization membership alone as sufficient authorization unless the implementation explicitly and correctly defines it that way.

---

## 2.3 Property → Unit Integrity

Every rental operation must resolve through the correct:

Property
→ Unit

A Unit must not belong to another Property.

A Tenant must not be attached to a Unit through an unrelated Property.

---

## 2.4 Tenant Integrity

Tenant relationships must remain consistent across:

Property
Unit
Tenant
Lease
Rent Schedule
Payment
Maintenance
Renewal / Move-out

Test for IDOR and cross-tenant access.

---

## 2.5 Lease Integrity

A Lease must connect the correct:

- property
- unit
- tenant
- owner
- management company, if applicable

Reject or prevent:

- wrong property/unit combinations
- wrong tenant
- unauthorized company
- invalid dates
- invalid rent
- invalid status transitions
- overlapping active leases where prohibited
